    An example

    One proposed mechanism, as MecCog represents it

    A genetic disease mechanism begins at the DNA stage and usually proceeds through a series of stages of biological organization, including perturbations at the protein, protein-protein, cellular and others, culminating in the organism stage disease phenotype. Each disease mechanism step is represented by a substate perturbation (SSP) produced by an activity represented by an oval mechanism module (MM). SSPs are shown as rectangles; MMs are labeled with the specific activity that produces the next SSP. Color coding indicates the schema builder's evidence assessment, from high - green, to lower - orange, to lowest - red. Black ovals indicate unknown steps. Select any step for more.
