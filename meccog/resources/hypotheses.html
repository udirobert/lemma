    Papers and Findings · Step 1

    The five hypotheses

    Mechanisms by which APOE4 has been proposed to alter endosome function, so increasing the risk of late-onset Alzheimer’s disease

    Before you start

    All five hypotheses concern proposed mechanisms by which the APOE4 genetic variant may increase the risk of late-onset Alzheimer's disease (LOAD).

    Hypotheses are precisely worded and include specifics that should not be ignored. However, findings that do not exactly fit the specifications are often still relevant. For example, all the hypotheses pertain to conditions in human brains, but most experiments are unavoidably made in model systems of one sort or another. Those findings are very much relevant. Later challenges will take experimental conditions into account in evaluating the strength of the evidence for or against a hypothesis. In this challenge, the task is to extract the experimental specifics needed for that.

    One reference is given per hypothesis, providing a literature source. Other papers also contain relevant evidence, and finding those is part of the challenge.

    H1

    H1: Lower endosome pH

    In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, for example astrocytes, APOE4 causes reduced endosome pH compared with APOE3.

    Starting reference. Prasad H, Rao R. Amyloid clearance defect in ApoE4 astrocytes is reversed by epigenetic correction of endosomal pH. Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A. 2018;115(28):E6640-E6649. PMID 29946028.

    H2

    H2: Impaired receptor recycling

    In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, for example astrocytes, APOE4 causes impaired ARF6-related recycling of receptors between the cell surface and the endosome, compared with APOE3.

    Starting reference. Rawat V, Wang S, Sima J, et al. ApoE4 Alters ABCA1 Membrane Trafficking in Astrocytes. J Neurosci. 2019;39(48):9611-9622. PMID 31641056.

    H3

    H3: APOE4 aggregation in the endo-lysosome

    In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, APOE4 undergoes increased aggregation in the endo-lysosome compared with APOE3.

    Starting reference. Kaji S, Berghoff SA, Spieth L, et al. Apolipoprotein E aggregation in microglia initiates Alzheimer's disease pathology by seeding beta-amyloidosis. Immunity. 2024;57(11):2651-2668.e12. PMID 39419029.

    H4

    H4: APOE4 adhesion to recycling receptors

    In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, APOE4 adheres more tightly to some recycling receptors than APOE3, impeding recycling.

    Starting reference. Xian X, Pohlkamp T, Durakoglugil MS, et al. Reversal of ApoE4-induced recycling block as a novel prevention approach for Alzheimer's disease. Elife. 2018;7:e40048. PMID 30375977.

    H5

    H5: Lipid metabolism and late endosome function

    In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, APOE4 disruption of lipid metabolism, compared with APOE3, results in impaired late endosome/lysosome function.

    Starting reference. Lee H, Cho S, Kim MJ, et al. ApoE4-dependent lysosomal cholesterol accumulation impairs mitochondrial homeostasis and oxidative phosphorylation in human astrocytes. Cell Rep. 2023;42(10):113183. PMID 37777962.
