    Shared resources

    The files you need for a submission. The challenge specifics page says how to use them.

    FileWhat it is
    submission_template.xlsxThe official template, with its Submission, Method and How to fill this in sheets.
    method_README_template.mdThe six sections of the method README. Publish it as README.md at the root of a public repository or archive.
    validate_submission.pyThe validator. Run it on your spreadsheet before you submit.
    Directness_of_evidence_v02.docxThe directness of evidence document, by John Moult: the five directness classes, the strength d, and the ABCA1 examples the challenge specifics page refers to.
    submission_example.xlsxA filled example, including the Method sheet. Its ABCA1 hypothesis is a worked example, not one of the five challenge hypotheses.
    Moult and Darden preprintMoult J, Darden L. Critical Assessment of Disease Mechanisms: APOE4 and Alzheimer's Disease. Preprint, 2025. Its Figure 1 is the mechanism drawn on the mechanism page, and the colors there follow that figure.

    Once your spreadsheet passes the validator, the team leader submits it on the submission page.

    Running the validator

    You need Python and the openpyxl package. Then run the validator on your spreadsheet:

    pip install openpyxl

    python validate_submission.py submission.xlsx
